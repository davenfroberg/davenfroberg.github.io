LLMs and Thought

May 12, 2026 · 6 min read

LLMs are changing the way we think and work, not necessarily for the better.


Really don't want this post to be a morally superior monologue, I just want to get my thoughts out there.

People using LLMs for formatting social media posts. And I'm not talking about influencers or social media managers, but random people using it to rewrite their words on a zero stakes comment or post description.

Walk around the library at UBC and see what you see. Every person using an LLM. Hard to judge cause I do the same but try to limit it.

People outsourcing learning to AI. Part of the learning is the process of finding the information yourself.
